2. KEY FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. HARDWARE-ACCELERATED 60 FPS CANVAS RENDERING
   - Custom drawWithCache Compose pipeline for maximum battery efficiency
   - Multi-layered dial:
     1. Outer Bezel with metallic gradient & ambient shadow
     2. 12 Major Hour Numerals / Markers
     3. 60 Precision Minute/Second Tick Indices
     4. Tapered Sword Hour Hand with depth shadow
     5. Precision Elongated Minute Hand
     6. Signature High-Contrast Needle Second Hand with counter-balance ring
     7. Metallic Center Cap & Pivot Ring

B. MOTION PHYSICS MODES
   1. Smooth Sweeping (Chronometer Mode):
      - Continuous angle calculation based on sub-second milliseconds:
        secondAngle = (seconds + millis / 1000f) * 6.0f
        minuteAngle = (minutes + seconds / 60f) * 6.0f
        hourAngle   = ((hours % 12) + minutes / 60f) * 30.0f
   2. Mechanical Ticking (Escapement Mode):
      - Discrete second-by-second step with subtle spring recoil bounce.

C. LOW-LATENCY TICK AUDIO
   - Generates realistic acoustic click sounds on each second transition using
     pure synthesized PCM pulses without needing external heavy audio files.

D. CONTROLS HUD & THEMES
   - Toggle: [Smooth Sweeping] vs [Mechanical Ticking]
   - Toggle: [Sound ON (Click)] vs [Muted]
   - Theme Switcher: Luxury Obsidian Black, Warm Ivory Cream, Modern Minimalist
   - Real-time Digital Time, Date & Timezone overlay
